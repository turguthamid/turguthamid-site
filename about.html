HOME/ABOUT ME

I think creatively. I manage systematically. I measure by results.

I began my professional career in media and communications. My experience in television and journalism taught me how to understand people, connect with audiences, communicate clear messages, and build compelling stories.

I later combined this background with marketing strategy, brand management, digital marketing, and business development.

Throughout my career, I have led marketing and communications across different companies. In some organisations, I built the marketing function from the ground up. In others, I developed existing systems to support new business objectives.

Today, I work across Azerbaijan, Georgia, Uzbekistan, Russia, Türkiye, Israel, the United Kingdom, Canada, and the United States, supporting brands in market development, localisation, and marketing communications.

Each market has its own consumer behaviour, media landscape, and commercial realities. My role is to maintain a consistent brand direction while developing the right approach for each local market.

I do not see marketing as advertising and communications alone. To me, marketing means understanding the market, identifying business opportunities, building a distinctive brand position, and creating a system that turns that position into measurable results.
